# Semantic & Metrics System

Own business meaning, contracts, metric governance and bounded semantic evaluation. Preserve every sibling and the shared AI-OS layer. All bundled approvals, health evidence and commerce rows are synthetic. No production definition is approved by the bundled registry.

Use `python3 -m semantic validate` and `python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -v` for required checks. Consumer consistency, timezone boundaries, grain/cardinality, version immutability and scenario disclosure must remain executable. Free-text diagnosis and recall are recorded practice; they require a human tutor for assessment.

Integrations are artifact contracts. Never describe exported adapter fixtures as live sibling connections. Live warehouse execution, ingestion, scheduling, general quality, operational monitoring and AI orchestration are outside this project.
