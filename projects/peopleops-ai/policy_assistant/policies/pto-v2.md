# PTO
## Requests
Submit PTO requests through the authenticated HR portal. Your manager reviews the request before leave is scheduled. Submit planned PTO at least five business days ahead where possible.

## Balances
For an individual PTO balance, consult the authenticated HR portal or contact People Operations privately. This policy does not establish an individual's available hours.
