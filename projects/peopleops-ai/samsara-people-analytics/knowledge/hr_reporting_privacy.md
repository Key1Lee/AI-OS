# HR reporting privacy

All records are fabricated. These are not Samsara's actual employee engagement results.
Report only company and department aggregates. Do not return individual answers, names, email addresses, or employee identities in chat or dashboards.
The raw local fixture endpoint contains fictional survey submission IDs for demonstration and reconciliation only.
The assistant uses the existing local Qwen model to interpret questions and select evidence. No external account, real Qualtrics login, paid API or cloud model is used. Calculations remain deterministic and independently verified.
Reject requests for individual employee results. PASS means the two deterministic calculators agree; it does not certify an actual survey or production system.
