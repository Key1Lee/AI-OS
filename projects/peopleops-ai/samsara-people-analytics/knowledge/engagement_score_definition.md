# Demo Company Engagement Score

The custom Demo Company Engagement Score is a fictional metric, not an official Samsara metric.
Respondent Score = ((QID1 + QID2 + QID3) / 3 - 1) × 25.
Company Engagement Score = the arithmetic mean of all valid respondent scores.
Every respondent has equal weight; do not average department averages.
The three-year overall score pools valid responses across the selected years with equal respondent weights.
Round only displayed results to two decimal places. Year-over-year change is the score minus the preceding calendar year's score in points, under the same department scope.
