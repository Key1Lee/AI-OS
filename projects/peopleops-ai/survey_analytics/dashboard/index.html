About these scores

Experience = mean engagement (1–5) × 20; its attainable range is 20–100. CSAT = 100 × satisfaction answers of 4 or 5 / answered questions. eNPS = 100 × (recommendation scores 9–10 minus scores 0–6) / answered questions; range −100 to +100. These describe survey sentiment, not individual employee performance.
