How to read these results

Experience score
Sum of answered 1–5 engagement scores ÷ answer count × 20. Reported to two decimals.
CSAT
Answers rated 4 or 5 ÷ all answered 1–5 satisfaction questions × 100.
eNPS
(Promoters − detractors) ÷ all answered 0–10 recommendation questions × 100.
Privacy & evidence
Only department aggregates are included. Slices require at least 5 completed surveys and 5 answers per question; all departments in this cohort qualify.

This is a fixed, synthetic demonstration, not a workforce assessment. No historical comparison, statistical significance, external benchmark, financial return or explanation of causes is inferred.
