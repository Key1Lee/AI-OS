BRD SUMMARY

The business case

A reviewer needs to understand how survey answers become trustworthy department scores. A chart alone cannot show whether records were duplicated, invalid answers were accepted, or missing questions changed the denominator. This workflow makes those steps inspectable.

The intended benefit is an easier, evidence-based review of data engineering decisions. The scope is one synthetic snapshot, local execution and department reporting. No measured labor saving, approved business funding or stakeholder signoff is claimed.

FIVE MINUTE INTERVIEW WALKTHROUGH

Show the outcome and the reasoning

  1. Open the guide 1 minute

    Explain the business problem and point to the six-stage architecture. State that all people and scores are fictional.

  2. Execute n8n 1 minute

    Open samsaraSurvey5000. Select Run synthetic survey from the trigger selector, then click Execute. Wait until all executed nodes complete.

  3. Inspect the evidence 1 minute

    Open Download synthetic responses, Bronze, Silver and Gold outputs. Show 5,000 records, the unchanged batch key and question-specific denominators. One n8n item can contain an array of 5,000 responses.

  4. Read the result 1 minute

    Open the dashboard. Filter Engineering, then switch to eNPS. Download the full-company CSV or HTML report.

  5. Prove repeatability 1 minute

    Run again and confirm the same cohort row counts. Explain the local error inbox and the production work still outstanding.

Expected local result: 5,000 accepted responses, zero quarantined cohort rows and ten department groups. Experience 75.99/100; CSAT 68.56%; eNPS +5.60. The question counts are 4,865, 4,828 and 4,879 respectively.

Important distinction: The earlier 500-request load test repeatedly downloaded a small fixture. This workflow collects 5,000 different fictional employees. One n8n item can hold all 5,000 responses.

PRODUCTION BOUNDARY

What still needs approval and engineering

  • Live vendor access needs approved credentials, an actual survey schema, bounded export polling, rate-limit handling and incremental synchronization.
  • Real employee data needs approved identity and authorization, secret management, transport protection, retention rules and privacy review. The public fake token is not enterprise security.
  • Production operation needs monitored infrastructure, backup and restore testing, external alert routing and measured performance objectives. SQLite and local inbox delivery are demonstration choices.
  • The configured ten minutes saved per run is an unmeasured estimate, not ROI evidence. MCP is enabled for synthetic workflows, but no authenticated client integration is claimed.

Source of truth: executable Python functions and the imported n8n JSON. This pack describes the working local baseline; it is not a production readiness signoff.
